# Public design foundations

Preserve the established Fort Worth Quality Roofing navy (#1c2a31), orange (#a94b25), soft orange (#edaa87), and white palette. Orange marks actions, not large background areas.

Use local system sans-serif fonts to avoid third-party font requests. Large balanced headlines, 68-character prose measures, 52px primary links, clear focus outlines, and rounded brand imagery carry the identity.

The promotional homepage pairs the approved mark with a clear live-site action. Service information uses a divided list, not repeated cards. Mobile stacks content in reading order. Synthetic examples use native disclosure controls. Do not introduce unverified claims or illustrative customer evidence.
